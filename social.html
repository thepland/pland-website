What is SMM?

SMM stands for Social Media Marketing.

It means using social media platforms like:

  • ✔ Instagram
  • ✔ Facebook
  • ✔ YouTube

to promote businesses or brands, run ads, increase followers, generate sales/leads, and build audience engagement.

Main Parts of SMM

  • ✔ Content Creation – posts, reels, stories, graphics
  • ✔ Paid Ads – Meta ads, Instagram ads, Facebook ads
  • ✔ Growth Strategy – hashtags, trends, engagement
  • ✔ Analytics – tracking reach, clicks, conversions

Example of SMM

A clothing store running Instagram reels and paid ads to sell products online is doing SMM.

About SMM

Since Meta ads and Instagram ads are part of social media promotion, SMM is the overall marketing field that includes those ad campaigns.
